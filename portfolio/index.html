About me

Architecture taught me to think in systems: how people move through a space, where friction lives, and how structure shapes behaviour.

I bring that same spatial logic to digital products — designing services and experiences that feel obvious in hindsight. With a Master of Architecture from the University of Sydney behind me, I bring a structural discipline to UX/UI design, service design and end-to-end design strategy.

At Mantel, I lead design on discovery and delivery engagements for clients like VicRoads, ANZ, Toll Group, Medibank, nib and Nine's Drive.com.au. My sweet spot is the messy front end of a problem: taking a complex, technical, many-stakeholdered challenge and turning it into something refined, human and buildable.

What I do best: experience mapping, end-to-end UX and service design, high-fidelity prototyping, user research, and getting rooms of stakeholders aligned and excited about the same thing. Always happy to talk design, discovery, or why buildings and apps have more in common than you'd think.
