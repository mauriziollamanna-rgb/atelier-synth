Stringline: Trades App UX/UI Redesign

From UI uplift to a ground-up rebuild of every flow, in time for MVP launch.

Client

Stringline

Type of work

Product & UX

Date

February – April 2024

My role

Product designer

Stringline is a startup helping sole traders and businesses in trades and services streamline their business: quoting, invoicing and scheduling.

The ask

With the app's release imminent, the original ask was a UI uplift of the existing screens. On closer examination, the flows demanded a lot of cognitive effort and exploration for users to achieve what they needed, so the engagement became a rebuild.

Working side-by-side with the client and their developer, flows were rebuilt from findings from user interviews and from stepping through user objectives together.

The outcome

  • 30+ flows reimagined and optimised across web and mobile.
  • 100+ screens designed for the MVP.
  • 38+ client playbacks as part of daily standups: decisions made with the founder, not for them.
  • 5 user tests to provide direction and validate flows before launch.
