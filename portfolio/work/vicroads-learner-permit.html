VicRoads — Learner Permit & Hazard Perception Test

Rethinking the learner permit test for mobile, with an AI study companion.

Client

VicRoads

Type of work

Strategy & product design

Date

November 2025 – February 2026

My role

Project lead · 8-week discovery

An eight-week discovery into the Learner Permit Test and Hazard Perception Test: what the experience should be, and which platform VicRoads should build it on.

The ask

Redesign the LPT/HPT experience for mobile, recommend a platform that could scale and pay its way, and make a case strong enough to get executive funding.

I led a team of three: me, another senior designer and a tech lead. We had daily standups with VicRoads, ran SME workshops, and played back to senior stakeholders every week.

My role

  • Research: ran six internal SME sessions and went through customer feedback. Language barriers and a clunky interface came up again and again.
  • Experience map: designed a six-stage target state, from first study session to licence issue.
  • UI design: high-fidelity, mobile-first designs for web and app.
  • Tech: worked with the tech leads so the designs fit the recommended Open edX hybrid platform.

The outcome

  • A future-state experience map, including longer-term ideas like AI cheating detection and online eye tests.
  • “Ask Lara”, an AI study companion that gives help specific to each page and adapts to how each learner studies.
  • Road safety features grounded in research, like spaced repetition to help people retain what they learn, and “Results Wrapped”, which shares what the community is getting right and wrong.
  • A white-label front end. The UI is headless and themed through configuration, so other states like NSW could use it.
  • VicRoads owns the IP. The open-source hybrid model saves money and opens up revenue from reselling.
