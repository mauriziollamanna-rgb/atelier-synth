VicRoads — Learner Permit & Hazard Perception Test

Reimagining how Victorians learn to drive — mobile-first, with an AI companion.

Client

VicRoads

Type of work

Strategy & product design

Date

November 2025 – February 2026

My role

Project lead · 8-week discovery

An 8-week discovery reimagining the Learner Permit Test and Hazard Perception Test experience, while identifying the future-proof platform the VicRoads test should move to.

The ask

Reimagine the LPT/HPT experience and recommend the right platform to design and build a mobile-first, frictionless test that is scalable, has a commercial outlook, and secures executive funding.

Our team was myself as project lead, another senior designer and a tech lead, working closely with the VicRoads team — daily standups, multiple SME workshops and a weekly playback with high-level stakeholders to keep alignment from beginning to end.

My role

  • Stakeholder & customer research: ran six internal SME sessions and analysed customer feedback to identify pain points like language barriers and interface friction.
  • Experience mapping: designed the Target State Experience — a six-stage strategic blueprint from research to final licence issuance.
  • Prototyping & UI design: delivered high-fidelity, mobile-first designs for both web and app.
  • Cross-functional collaboration: partnered with technical leads to keep design solutions viable within the recommended Open edX hybrid platform.

The outcome

  • The future-state experience map: a strategic blueprint for the ideal journey, with future ideas like AI anti-cheat detection and online eye tests.
  • “Ask Lara” AI companion: a smart assistant providing continuous learning, page-specific support and training that adapts to individual learning styles.
  • Road safety uplift: research-backed features like “Results Wrapped” — turning personal assessment into community learning — and spaced repetition to combat knowledge decay.
  • Scalable, white-label framework: a headless UI architecture with configuration-driven theming, ready to white-label for other jurisdictions like Service NSW.
  • Commercial sovereignty: the open-source hybrid model gives VicRoads full IP ownership — significant cost savings and new revenue streams through reselling.
