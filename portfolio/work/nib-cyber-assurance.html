nib: Continuous Cyber Assurance

Turning cyber-risk data into a conversation executives can actually have.

Client

nib

Type of work

Product & research

Date

September – November 2023

My role

Associate designer

The nib cyber team were struggling to communicate cyber risks to the business and its executives. They wanted a continuous assurance platform, backed by data, that could carry the conversation about the effectiveness of cyber controls.

The ask

Design the dashboard experience that would let a technical security team brief non-technical executives, without losing the data underneath.

My role

  • Performed user interviews across the security team and its stakeholders.
  • Created journey maps, personas and business use cases for the dashboard.
  • Built the story map and feature list, divided into releases.

The outcome

The nib cyber team now have a cyber security dashboard backed by the data within their organisation, used to communicate the status of cyber controls to the executive.
