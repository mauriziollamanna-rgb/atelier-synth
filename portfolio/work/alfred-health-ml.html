Alfred Health — ML for Blood Transfusion Prediction

Making a machine-learning model usable — and fundable.

Client

Alfred Health

Type of work

UX & research

Date

June – July 2024

My role

UX designer

Alfred Health engaged Mantel to explore whether a machine-learning model could predict which patients would need a red blood cell transfusion.

The ask

The project involved uplifting the data analytics team's approach to ML Ops and providing a roadmap to migrate them from a bespoke, largely manual solution onto a scalable Databricks implementation.

The design challenge: make a highly technical prediction model legible and trustworthy for the clinicians and analysts who would rely on it.

My role

  • Defined the problem together with the client.
  • Understood the user journey through interviews with key users.
  • Facilitated an ideation workshop across clinical and data stakeholders.
  • Delivered high-fidelity prototypes used to secure future funding.

The outcome

High-fidelity prototypes that showed what the prediction service could look like in clinicians' hands — clear enough to align the team and compelling enough to help secure future funding for the program.
