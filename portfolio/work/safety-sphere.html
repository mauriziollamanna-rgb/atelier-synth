Safety Sphere — Vision Pro Demo

Mantel's first foray into designing for Apple Vision Pro.

Client

Mantel — internal R&D

Type of work

Spatial computing

Date

May – June 2024

My role

Designer

An internal initiative to uplift Mantel's capability on Apple Vision Pro — our first demo app built to show clients what we can design and deliver in spatial computing.

The ask

Before ANZ, before client work in visionOS, we needed our own proof: a working demo experience that showed we could take spatial computing from idea to hardware.

My role

  • Aligned internal stakeholders through an ideation workshop.
  • Created the storyboard for the proposed experience.
  • Delivered high-fidelity Vision Pro designs.
  • Collaborated with the development team to ship a working demo.

The outcome

A working Vision Pro demo, delivered with our development team — the capability foundation that later won and shaped client engagements in spatial computing, including ANZ's Digital Expo.
