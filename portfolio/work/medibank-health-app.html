Medibank — Preventative Health App

What if your body came with a service manual?

Client

Medibank Ventures

Type of work

Product & UX

Date

April – May 2024

My role

UX designer · 3-week engagement

Medibank Ventures had an idea: what if your body came with a service manual? They brought me in to help show what a preventative health service could look like.

The ask

Over three weeks I worked with the Lead Product Designer and a GP from MyHealth Medical Centre to make the idea concrete enough to get stakeholders behind it.

What I made

  • A journey map that follows someone from their health “service manual” to a booked preventative appointment.
  • High-fidelity, animated screens for the whole journey, from learning about your health to booking a GP.
  • The company-wide presentation used to bring more stakeholders on board.

The outcome

A clickable, animated prototype, presented company-wide, that made an abstract idea feel like a product Medibank could build.
