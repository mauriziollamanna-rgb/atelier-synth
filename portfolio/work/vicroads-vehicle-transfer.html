VicRoads — Vehicle Transfer Service

Escrow-backed vehicle transfers, designed end to end and proven buildable.

Client

VicRoads

Type of work

Service & product design

Date

April – July 2026

My role

Design lead · 10-week discovery

A 10-week discovery to prove feasibility, size the effort and de-risk a production-grade Vehicle Transfer Service — the full buyer and seller experience through myVicRoads, wired into Shaype's escrow platform.

The ask

VicRoads wanted to know whether an escrow-backed vehicle transfer service could actually be built — and what it would take. Over ten weeks we designed and prototyped the full buyer/seller experience, sized the build effort, and packaged everything so it could go straight into a future business case.

I led the design alongside three Lead Engineers, working shoulder-to-shoulder with VicRoads Product and Shaype's integration team.

My role

  • Experience mapping: built the VTS Experience Map with VicRoads — a visual, high-level journey covering both buyer and seller, showing the flexibility in the overall design.
  • Golden path & UX design: designed the primary, secondary and tertiary paths across the end-to-end journey, alongside alternate flows and error scenarios.
  • Prototyping & demo: collaborated on a React Native demo — a key artefact for the future business case.
  • Requirements & estimation: gathered requirements across payments and the end-to-end design, aligning VicRoads and Shaype on edge cases upfront so nothing bites in the build phase.
  • Documentation for handover: turned ten weeks of decisions into build-ready epic documentation — flows, rules and error states.

The outcome

  • Proved the hardest part works: designed and validated the Shaype escrow model end to end — funds locked in a joint account and split automatically across Seller, SRO, VicRoads and VTS Premium on handover.
  • Foundational documentation: five epics covering flow steps, alternate flows and error scenarios, so the build team can hit the ground running.
  • Made it real, not theoretical: a working React Native demo turned an abstract pitch into something stakeholders could hold and click through, surfacing UX and technical decisions while they were still cheap to change.
  • De-risked a multi-million dollar decision: turned an ambition into a defensible effort-to-build number, with a recommended plan and team structure attached.
