VicRoads — Vehicle Transfer Service

Designing a safer way to buy and sell a used car in Victoria, with payments held in escrow.

Client

VicRoads

Type of work

Service & product design

Date

April – July 2026

My role

Design lead · 10-week discovery

VicRoads wanted to know if an escrow-backed vehicle transfer service could be built, and what it would cost. We spent ten weeks finding out, designing the buyer and seller experience in myVicRoads and connecting it to Shaype's escrow platform.

The ask

Prove the service was feasible, estimate the build, and give VicRoads enough material to take into a business case.

I led design alongside three lead engineers, working closely with VicRoads Product and Shaype's integration team.

My role

  • Experience map: mapped the buyer and seller journeys with VicRoads, so everyone could see the whole service on one page.
  • Journeys and edge cases: designed the primary, secondary and tertiary paths, plus the alternate flows and error states.
  • Demo: worked with the engineers on a React Native demo for the business case.
  • Requirements: worked through payments and edge cases with VicRoads and Shaype early, while they were still cheap to change.
  • Handover: wrote up the flows, rules and error states as epics the build team can pick up directly.

The outcome

  • The escrow model works. Funds sit in a joint account and are split automatically between the seller, SRO, VicRoads and VTS Premium at handover. We designed and tested this end to end.
  • Five build-ready epics covering flow steps, alternate flows and error scenarios.
  • A demo people could use. Stakeholders could click through the service themselves, which brought up UX and technical questions early.
  • A build estimate VicRoads can defend, with a recommended plan and team structure.
