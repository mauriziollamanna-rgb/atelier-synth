Belong — North Star Discovery

A North Star for Belong's order-to-activate journey, and a shift toward designing around customers.

Client

Belong

Type of work

Service design & strategy

Date

November 2022 – April 2023

My role

Experience designer

Belong asked Mantel for a North Star vision of the order-to-activate experience, to help the business put customers at the centre of how it designs and delivers.

The ask

Help Belong agree on what a great order-to-activate journey looks like, and feel confident about it.

Our approach

  • Research: industry and competitor analysis, stakeholder workshops, and quantitative and qualitative customer research.
  • Define: mapped the problems and opportunities in a Vision Experience Map.
  • Ideate: workshops, concept generation and storyboards.
  • Prototype: wireframes and prototypes for the ideas with the most business value.
  • Test: several rounds of concept and usability testing.

The outcome

  • Experience principles for every customer interaction, written with the business and tested with customers.
  • The Vision Experience Map: the ideal order-to-activate journey from start to finish, with feature summaries, research and future opportunities. Belong can keep updating it.
  • Tested concepts and designs, including detailed designs for the first release of eSIM ordering and activation. Other key journeys followed into delivery from there.
