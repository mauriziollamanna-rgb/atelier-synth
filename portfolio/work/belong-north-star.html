Belong: North Star Discovery

A North Star vision that pivoted Belong toward customer-centric design and delivery.

Client

Belong

Type of work

Service design & strategy

Date

November 2022 – April 2023

My role

Experience designer

Belong engaged Mantel to create a North Star vision for the order-to-activate customer experience, helping pivot the organisation towards a more customer-centric approach to design and delivery.

The ask

Help Belong gain confidence and alignment on what an ideal, customer-centric order-to-activate journey looks like.

Our approach

  • Empathise: extensive research, industry and competitor analysis, stakeholder workshops, and quantitative and qualitative customer research.
  • Define: mapped challenges and opportunities through a Vision Experience Map.
  • Ideate: ideation workshops, concept canvassing, concept generation and storyboarding.
  • Prototype: wireframed and prototyped the high-value business ideas.
  • Validate: multiple rounds of concept and usability testing.

The outcome

  • Experience principles: guiding principles that underpin and measure every customer interaction with Belong, created with the business and validated through customer testing.
  • The Vision Experience Map: a holistic, end-to-end view of the ideal order-to-activate journey: feature summaries, supporting research and future opportunities. Designed as a living document that matures as Belong does.
  • Validated concepts and designs: future-state concepts plus granular designs for the first release of the eSIM ordering and activation experience, the foundation for other key journeys to go into delivery.
