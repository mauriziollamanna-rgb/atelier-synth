Toll Group — MoveMaestro Future Vision

A target-state vision for relocations that aligned four user groups around one experience.

Client

Toll Group

Type of work

Service design & strategy

Date

January – March 2025

My role

Experience designer

Toll Transitions manages employee and family relocations for the ADF, Home Affairs and corporate businesses. Existing systems were insufficient — posing technical and commercial risks to future growth.

The ask

Create a target-state vision and ideal moving experience for Toll customers, clients, employees and suppliers using MoveMaestro.

Our approach

  • Capture the vision: validated the target-state experience through internal and customer interviews, addressing pain points and opportunities.
  • Create the experience: low-fidelity designs for the Customer, Toll, Client and Supplier flows, aligned with Toll SMEs.
  • Bring it to life: high-fidelity screens of the target state, technology recommendations, and a high-level roadmap.

The outcome

  • The vision map: gave stakeholders a clear strategic direction and a holistic view of every interaction — the anchor for alignment.
  • The service & journey map: enabled practical alignment on improvements, supported informed decision-making and drove strategic discussions.
  • High-fidelity prototype & screens: a tangible representation of the future experience that generated real excitement among clients.
