ANZ — Digital Expo on Apple Vision Pro

One of the first banking experiences built for Apple Vision Pro in Australia.

Client

ANZ

Type of work

Spatial computing

Date

June – July 2025

My role

Experience designer

ANZ Institutional runs an expo to show key clients its digital roadmap. They asked Mantel to rethink it.

The problem

ANZ works across 32 markets and has more than 8.5 million customers. A physical expo is expensive to run and only reaches the people in the room. ANZ wanted something it could take anywhere.

The design

We designed an interactive expo for Apple Vision Pro:

  • Guided scenarios, presented by an AI avatar.
  • A 3D map of the expo floor.
  • A virtual theatrette where remote clients can watch the keynotes.

It can be adapted for different pitches, events and markets.

The outcome

  • More engaging than a booth. Clients work through real examples rather than reading a stand.
  • Shown at SIBOS. ANZ became the first bank to complete a proof of concept on the headset.
  • Cheaper to run. ANZ can hold the expo anywhere, and estimates savings of up to $200,000 per physical event.
  • A competitive edge that could help ANZ in the Peter Lee survey.
