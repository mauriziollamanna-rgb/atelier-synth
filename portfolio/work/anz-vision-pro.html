ANZ: Digital Expo on Apple Vision Pro

One of Australia's first banking experiences in spatial computing.

Client

ANZ

Type of work

Spatial computing

Date

June – July 2025

My role

Experience designer

ANZ's institutional arm is the No. 1 institutional bank across Australia and New Zealand. To better engage key banking clients and showcase its digital roadmap, ANZ asked Mantel to reimagine its traditional banking expo.

The problem

As one of Australia's Big Four banks, ANZ operates across 32 markets and serves over 8.5 million customers. Its traditional expo format carried heavy logistical and cost burdens, and could only reach the people physically in the room. The goal: transform the event into a scalable, interactive experience.

The design

We created a pioneering interactive experience for the Apple Vision Pro headset:

  • Hyper-engaging, guided scenarios led by an AI avatar.
  • A dynamic Interactive Expo Map: a 3D representation of the event.
  • A Virtual Theatrette where remote customers watch keynote speakers.

The entire concept was designed to be scalable and adaptable: specific pitches, different expos, any market.

The outcome

  • Enhanced customer experience: real-life examples and guided scenarios lift engagement beyond what a physical booth can do.
  • Broader reach: the interactive demo was showcased at the SIBOS conference, making ANZ the first bank to complete a proof of concept with the technology.
  • Scalable framework: ANZ can now run the expo anytime, anywhere, with potential savings of up to $200,000 per physical event.
  • Strategic advantage: a “game changer” platform with competitive upside, including a potential Peter Lee survey win.
