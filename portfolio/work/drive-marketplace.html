Drive.com.au: Marketplace Discovery

Coaching Nine's product team through design sprints for the marketplace's next two years.

Client

Nine (Drive.com.au)

Type of work

Research & strategy

Date

June – September 2023

My role

Sprint lead & coach · 4-week discovery

Drive is Australia's largest automotive content network, owned by Nine Entertainment. Cars 4 Sale and Sell My Car are the consumer-facing sections crucial to generating green shoots for the marketplace business.

The ask

A four-week discovery to support Drive's UX research and design capability, partnering with their UI design and product team to reimagine the customer experience and features of the marketplace for the next one to two years.

The cross-functional team ran deep: CFO, Head of Product, a Senior Product Manager, Product Lead, digital designers and UX/UI designers.

My role

  • Planned, executed and coached the Drive team through agile design sprints, modelled on the Google Design Sprint methodology.
  • Supported production of two high-fidelity responsive web prototypes.
  • Conducted an industry review of key competitors to identify optimal UX patterns.
  • Led research techniques and ideation workshops, coaching the team on human-centred methods.
  • Planned and executed two rounds of moderated user research: synthesis, design recommendations and stakeholder playbacks.
  • Interviewed key SMEs for contextual understanding and stakeholder alignment.

The outcome

Hero journeys for the two marketplace products (ideal customer experiences and further opportunities) plus user archetypes grounded in the research, setting Drive's product direction for the next two years.
